If you use Landsat, consider the following:
1. Use the latest Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 Surface Reflectance datasets only:
    LANDSAT/LT04/C02/T1_L2, LANDSAT/LT05/C02/T1_L2, LANDSAT/LE07/C02/T1_L2, LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2, LANDSAT/LC09/C02/T1_L2.
2. Band map:
- TM/ETM+ (LT04/LT05/LE07):  RED=SR_B3, GREEN=SR_B2, NIR=SR_B4, SWIR1=SR_B5, SWIR2=SR_B7
- OLI/OLI-2 (LC08/LC09):     RED=SR_B4, GREEN=SR_B3, NIR=SR_B5, SWIR1=SR_B6, SWIR2=SR_B7
